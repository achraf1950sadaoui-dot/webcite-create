# 🏢 Email Outreach — Dubai Real Estate Agencies

Email suits **professional, office-based businesses** (real estate agencies,
clinics, law firms) — they check email, unlike no-website restaurants/salons
(WhatsApp/Instagram for those).

> ⚠️ Never bulk-blast 20 cold emails from a raw @gmail — Google flags it and can
> suspend the account. Create **drafts** (reviewed, then sent), pace 10–20/day,
> warm up a proper domain. See `SEND-AUTOMATION.md`.

---

## Where to get real agency emails (free)

- **Dubai Land Department** — licensed real-estate brokers list
- **Bayut** / **Property Finder** — "Find an agency / broker" pages
- **Yellow Pages UAE** (yellowpages-uae.com) — real-estate brokerage category
- **Google Maps** — search `real estate agency Business Bay` etc., check contact

Collect: agency name · email · area · website status. Paste into the Lead Manager.

## The pitch angle for agencies

Most agencies already have a company site — so don't sell "you have no website".
Sell **conversion**: dedicated **campaign landing pages** for their Facebook/Google
property ads, with a WhatsApp lead form, fast load, and mobile-first design. That's
a fresh need even for agencies that have a main site.

## Reply handling

- **"How much?"** → AED [1,500–3,000] per landing page, or [X]/mo for ongoing pages
  + edits. Want a quick breakdown or a call?
- **"Send the preview"** → build it (use `demo-restaurant`/`demo-salon` as a style
  reference; a property landing page demo can be spun up on request), send the link.
- **"We have a site"** → "Perfect — this is a campaign landing page for your ads, not
  a replacement. Want to see one built for [Agency]?"
