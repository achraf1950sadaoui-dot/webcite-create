# 🎯 Playbook — 3 Website Clients / Week (Dubai, UAE)

Target market: **Dubai / UAE small businesses** — restaurants first, then salons,
clinics, gyms, real-estate, boutiques. Sell in **English**. Channels: **cold email
+ WhatsApp + Instagram DM**.

---

## The core idea: sell with a *preview*, not a pitch

Everyone ignores "I build websites" emails. Nobody ignores **"I already built you one — here's the link."**

The workflow:
1. **Find** a business with a weak or missing website.
2. **Claude generates a personalized preview** — their real name, tagline, a few real
   menu items / services, their colours. (Ask me — this takes ~10 min per prospect.)
3. **You send** the preview link by email + WhatsApp/IG.
4. **Follow up** twice.
5. **Close**: one-time build fee + optional monthly care plan.

This is *value-first*. It's specific, it's real, and it doesn't look like spam —
which is exactly the "not seen as a scam" problem you raised.

## Who to target (best-fit signals)

Go after businesses that **have money but a weak web presence**:

✅ **Strong fit**
- Active Instagram (posting, real photos, 1k+ followers) **but** no website, or a
  broken/ugly one, or only a Linktree.
- Listed on Talabat / Deliveroo / Zomato but no own site.
- Good Google rating (4.3★+) with many reviews = real, busy business.
- New openings (they need everything).

❌ **Skip**
- Big chains (they have agencies).
- Dead businesses (no recent posts, <3.8★, few reviews).
- Places with an already-great modern site.

## Where to find them (Dubai)

1. **Google Maps** — search `restaurants DIFC`, `cafe JLT`, `salon Business Bay`,
   `clinic Marina`. Open each → check "Website" field. **No website = hot lead.**
2. **Instagram** — search `#dubairestaurant`, `#dxbfoodie`, location tags. Check the
   bio link. Linktree / no link = lead.
3. **Talabat / Deliveroo** — lots of restaurants there have no site of their own.
4. **Time Out Dubai / new-opening blogs** — fresh businesses that need a site now.

For each lead, capture: **name · category · area · Instagram · phone/WhatsApp ·
email · website status**. Put it in `pipeline-tracker.csv`.

## Anti-scam checklist (so you get replies, not spam-flags)

- [ ] **Own domain email** (you@youragency.com) — never a raw @gmail for cold email. Warm it up first (send/receive normally for 1–2 weeks, ramp volume slowly).
- [ ] **One clean link** to a preview on *your* domain. No attachments, no link shorteners.
- [ ] **Personalized**: use their real name in subject + first line. Generic = spam.
- [ ] **Short**: 4–6 lines. A wall of text screams scam.
- [ ] **Real signature**: your name, agency, UAE phone/WhatsApp, website.
- [ ] **Easy out**: "reply 'no' and I won't follow up." (Also legally cleaner.)
- [ ] **Volume discipline**: <30–40 cold emails/day per inbox at first. Blasting = blacklist.
- [ ] Prefer **WhatsApp/IG for restaurants** — they live there; reply rates beat email.
