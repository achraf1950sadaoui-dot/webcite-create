# ✉️ Cold Email Sequences (Dubai · English)

Replace `[…]` before sending. Keep it short, personal, one link. Send from your
own-domain email. Always send the WhatsApp/IG version too — restaurants reply there faster.

---

## SEQUENCE A — "Preview already built" (highest converting) ⭐

Use when Claude has built a personalized preview for the prospect.

### Email 1 — Day 0

**Subject:** A new website for [Restaurant Name] (built it for you)

> Hi [First name / Team],
>
> I had dinner idea about [Restaurant Name] and noticed you don't have a website yet
> (just Instagram). So I built you a quick preview of what one could look like:
>
> 👉 [preview link]
>
> It's mobile-ready, takes reservations straight to your WhatsApp, and shows your menu.
> Free to look — if you like it, I can have the real one live this week.
>
> Want me to walk you through it?
>
> [Your name] · [Agency] · Dubai
> 📱 WhatsApp: [number] · [youragency.com]
>
> *Not interested? Reply "no" and I won't follow up.*

### Email 2 — Day 3 (if no reply)

**Subject:** Re: A new website for [Restaurant Name]

> Hi [First name],
>
> Did the preview come through? 👉 [preview link]
>
> One thing it would fix right away: guests searching "[Restaurant Name] Dubai" on
> Google land on nothing right now — a site captures those bookings.
>
> Happy to change the colours/photos to match your brand. 2-minute call?
>
> [Your name]

### Email 3 — Day 7 (breakup)

**Subject:** Should I close the preview for [Restaurant Name]?

> Hi [First name],
>
> I'll take the preview down at the end of the week unless you'd like to keep it.
> If now's not the time, no problem — just let me know and I'll leave you to it.
>
> Either way, the preview is yours to keep: [preview link]
>
> [Your name]

## Subject-line bank (A/B test these)

- A new website for [Restaurant Name] (built it for you)
- [First name] — made you something
- [Restaurant Name] + a website = more bookings
- Quick preview for [Restaurant Name] 👀
- Found [Restaurant Name] on Google — one thing missing
- 2 minutes for [Restaurant Name]?

**Rules:** put their name in the subject · lowercase feels human · no ALL CAPS, no "$$$",
no "FREE!!!", no spammy punctuation (those trip spam filters).

---

## Reply handling

**"How much?"**
> Great question! It's AED [1,500–3,000] one-time depending on the sections you want,
> and I include the first month of hosting free. Want me to send a quick breakdown, or
> easier to hop on a 5-min call?

**"Send me more info"**
> Of course — here's the preview again [link] and a 60-second walkthrough [video/loom].
> If it looks right, I can start today and have it live by [day].

**"We're too busy right now"**
> Totally understand. The preview stays up for you [link]. Want me to check back in
> [2 weeks]? No pressure either way.

**"We already have someone / a site"**
> No worries at all! If you ever want a second opinion or a refresh, I'm here.
> Mind if I send one idea that could get you more bookings?

---

## Guardrails

- Everything true. Never invent awards, fake reviews, or "clients said…".
- One link. No attachments (they trip filters and look risky).
- ≤ 30–40 cold emails/day/inbox while your domain is new.
- Honour every opt-out immediately.
