# 💬 WhatsApp / Instagram DM Scripts (Dubai · English)

For **restaurants and local businesses, WhatsApp + Instagram beat email.** They live
there and reply in minutes. Keep messages short, human, and lowercase-friendly. Never
send a wall of text. One message → wait → then the link.

> ⚠️ Use WhatsApp Business, keep volume human (don't blast identical messages to 100
> numbers in an hour — you'll get flagged). Personalize each one.

---

## Opening (with a preview ready) ⭐

**Message 1** — hook only, no link yet:
> Hi [Restaurant Name] team 👋 I'm [Your name], a web designer in Dubai. Big fan of
> your [dish/place] — quick question, do you have a website or just Instagram?

*(Wait for reply. If they answer, or after a few hours, send Message 2.)*

**Message 2** — the preview:
> I actually built you a free preview of what a site could look like 👇
> [preview link]
> It's mobile, shows your menu, and takes table bookings straight to your WhatsApp.
> If you like it I can make it live this week 🙌

---

## Opening (no preview — cold)

> Hi [Restaurant Name] 👋 I design websites for Dubai restaurants — clean, mobile, with
> WhatsApp booking. Here's a live example: [demo link]
> Want me to build a free preview with *your* name on it so you can see it first? Takes me a day 🙂

## Instagram DM (shorter)

> Love what you're doing at [Restaurant Name] 🔥 noticed there's no website in your bio —
> just built a quick preview of one for you, want me to send it? 👀

## Follow-ups

**Day 2:**
> Hey [First name], did you get a chance to peek at the preview? [link] 🙂 happy to tweak
> the colours/photos to match your brand.

**Day 5:**
> No worries if the timing's off! The preview's yours to keep 👇 [link] — want me to check
> back next week or leave it with you?

---

## Objection handling

**"How much?"**
> One-time AED [1,500–3,000] depending on the sections, first month hosting on me. Want the
> quick breakdown?

**"Can you add online ordering / delivery?"**
> 100%. I can wire it to WhatsApp or link Talabat/Deliveroo, or build full ordering. Let's
> pick what fits — 5-min call?

**"Is it monthly?"**
> The build is one-time. Hosting + edits are an optional AED [300–600]/mo care plan — or you
> can self-host, your call.

**"Send me a call later"**
> Perfect — what time works? I'll call you on WhatsApp. Meanwhile here's the preview: [link]

**"How do I know it's real?"**
> Totally fair 🙂 here's my site [youragency.com], my Instagram [@handle], and the live preview
> I made for you [link]. Happy to do a quick video call so you see my face too.

---

## Closing → getting paid

> Awesome, let's do it 🙌 To start I'll need: (1) your logo/photos, (2) your menu, (3) your
> WhatsApp number for bookings. Build fee is AED [amount] — [50% to start / on delivery].
> I'll have a first version for you by [day]. Sound good?

**Payment (Dubai):** bank transfer, or a payment link (Stripe/Telr/Ziina/PayTabs).
Take **50% upfront** on bigger builds. For AED 1,500 starters, on-delivery is fine.

---

## Do / Don't

✅ One short message first, link second · personalize every send · reply fast · use voice
notes for warmth · keep promises on timing.

❌ No paragraph-long first message · no 10 links · no fake urgency/lies · no identical blast
to 100 numbers · never argue with a "no" — leave the door open.
