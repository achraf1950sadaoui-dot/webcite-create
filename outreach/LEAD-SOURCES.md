# 📍 Where to Find Real Dubai Leads (feeds the Lead Manager)

The Lead Manager (`lead-manager.html`) scores and tracks leads — but **you** collect the
real ones (I won't invent fake businesses; wrong data burns your outreach). Here's the
fast, repeatable way to gather 8–10 solid leads per day.

---

## Method 1 — Google Maps (best for "no website" signal) ⭐

1. Open Google Maps. Search an **area + category**, e.g.:
   - `restaurants in JLT`  ·  `cafe Dubai Marina`  ·  `salon Business Bay`
   - `dental clinic JVC`  ·  `gym Al Barsha`  ·  `boutique City Walk`
2. Click a business. In the panel, look for the **"Website"** button.
   - **No Website button = 🔥 hot lead.**
   - Has a Linktree / Instagram link instead = still hot.
3. Grab: **name, area, phone, rating, website status**. Note their Instagram from the panel.
4. Add it in the tool (single form) or collect a batch and paste (Method below).

> Do ~2 areas per day. Rotate: Marina, JLT, Business Bay, JVC, Al Barsha, DIFC, Downtown,
> Deira, Jumeirah, City Walk, Dubai Hills.

---

## Method 2 — Instagram (best for restaurants/cafes/salons)

1. Search hashtags/locations: `#dubairestaurant` `#dxbfoodie` `#dubaisalon` `#dubaicafe`,
   or tap a location tag (e.g. "Dubai Marina").
2. Open active accounts (recent posts, real photos, 1k+ followers).
3. Check the **bio link**: none, or a Linktree, = lead. A real website = usually skip.
4. Grab handle + any WhatsApp/email in the bio.

---

## Method 3 — Delivery apps & directories

- **Talabat / Deliveroo / Zomato** — many restaurants list there but own **no website**.
- **Google** `restaurants Dubai site:instagram.com` or area guides on **Time Out Dubai**,
  **Visit Dubai**, new-opening blogs → fresh businesses that need everything.

---

## What makes a lead "hot" (the tool scores this for you)

| Signal | Why |
|--------|-----|
| No website / Linktree / broken | Your whole pitch — they need what you sell |
| Active Instagram, real photos | They care about image → will pay for a good site |
| Google rating 4.3★+ with reviews | Real, busy, has cash flow |
| Restaurant / cafe / salon / clinic / gym | Highest-converting categories |
| New opening | Needs everything, fewer competitors pitching |

**Skip:** big chains, dead pages (<3.8★, no recent posts), places with an already-modern site.

## Feeding the tool fast — paste format

In the Lead Manager → **"Paste from Google Maps"** tab, one lead per line:

```
Name | Category | Area | @instagram | phone | email | websiteStatus
```

Example:
```
Zaytoun Kitchen | Restaurant | JLT | @zaytoundxb | +9715012345 |  | None
Bloom Cafe | Cafe | Marina | @bloomcafe |  | hi@bloom.ae | Linktree
Glow Beauty | Salon | Business Bay | @glowdxb | +9715022334 |  | Broken
```

- Only **Name** is required. Keep the `|` separators; leave a field blank if unknown.
- `websiteStatus` = one of: `None` · `Linktree` · `Broken` · `Basic` · `Good`.
