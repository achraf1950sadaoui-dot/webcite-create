Our Philosophy

An ode to the
seven emirates.

NÛR — meaning light in Arabic — is the culinary vision of Chef Yasmin Al-Hashimi. Each course is a study in contrast: the heat of the dunes and the chill of the Gulf, the heritage of bedouin spice and the precision of Parisian technique.

Twelve tables. One chef's counter. Zero compromises.

Behind the Counter

Twelve years of obsession.

Trained at L'Arpège in Paris and Noma in Copenhagen, Chef Yasmin returned to Dubai with a singular question: what would the cuisine of the Arabian peninsula taste like if it had never paused to look elsewhere?

NÛR is the answer — a kitchen rooted in ancestral spice, sharpened by Nordic discipline, and plated with Parisian restraint.

"We do not chase trends. We chase memory."
