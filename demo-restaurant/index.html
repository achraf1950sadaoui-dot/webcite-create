Our Story

Recipes carried across generations.

Maison Noor began with a grandmother's spice tin and a simple belief: that the best conversations happen over food meant to be shared. Our kitchen brings the soul of Beirut, Aleppo and Amman to a modern Dubai table.

Every dish is grilled over natural charcoal, seasoned by hand, and plated to gather people around it. No shortcuts — just fire, spice, and hospitality.

— Chef Layla Noor
